# Veil Runner
Inspiration image: 39180c08

## Fantasy and verb
Become the moving dark silhouette inside an impossible pearl city. **Read the haze, then run through it.** Enormous latticed lantern-globes hover above wet paving; ghosts drift past Gothic ribs. Solid destinations have a quiet pulsing hem and grounded shadow; apparitions rise and dissolve. This is a visual discrimination toy, with no claim of eyesight improvement.

## Genre and loop
First-person open-world perception / parkour arcade runner.
**Spot a grounded silhouette → run, jump, slide and shatter toward it → send a silver ripple across the city → earn resonance → grow a permanent movement web → reach elevated, subtler districts.** No gates requiring collectible counts. The whole city is freely approachable; upgrades make upper routes practical.

## Immediate play
Load directly into the plaza facing a nearby dark coat. WASD works immediately; click captures look. A small icon guide and a pulsing ground trail teach the first approach. Approaching silhouettes awards resonance; a new target appears in another district. Shift accelerates, Space jumps, C slides, click pulses/shatters fragile screens. Missed apparitions restore a little contrast and show a diffuse ripple, without losing currency. A visibility reset is always available. No intro scene.

## Long progression
Six interconnected branches, eight tiers apiece, purchased with route earnings. Stride increases speed and foot trails; Flow extends slides and unlocks wall-running; Lift improves jumps, unlocks double jumps and vault assistance; Pulse increases smash radius and fragment counts; Sight lengthens hint ripples and adds direction cues; Echo awakens passive district income. Tier prerequisites branch through the web. Costs grow across 48 purchases, with first major unlocks in the first few minutes. Movement changes open cathedral balconies, maintenance bridges, flooded plazas and spectral gardens; these distinguish vertical silhouettes, reflection, slow sway and lattice geometry. Globe filigree and colored trails grow with power. Save currency, upgrades, contrast limits and best quick times locally; offline income capped at eight hours.

## Contrast and quick play
Main mode gently lowers contrast after successful routes, within a player-adjustable floor. Misses restore it; V immediately restores visibility. Controls always remain readable. A daily seeded 60-second quick route uses a fixed personal contrast, no upgrades, instant R retry, with time and detection accuracy scored separately. Tab opens the skill web; Escape/settings permit quality, contrast and audio adjustment.

## Feel and world
Forgiving coyote jumps, camera lean, landing kick, sliding streaks, synthesized footsteps and bell harmonics. Crystal screens break into pooled instanced shards; impacts briefly expose space with bright ripples and numbers. Ghosts and debris are instanced, with distance fading. World-space triplanar procedural stone shader avoids stretched terrain textures. Low/medium/high presets bound pixel ratio, crowds and geometry; auto detects modest hardware.

## Why keep playing?
Minute 1: cross the image, burst through crystal, hear a bell and chase the next ripple.
Minute 10: double-jump and wall-run between elevated routes, build a long streak, buy visibly stronger pulse tiers.
Minute 30: combine all movement branches, explore district-specific cues and continue pushing personal contrast limits or daily times. The skill web supplies long progress without prestige resets.

## Scope and validation
Procedural architecture, detailed lathed coat figure, animated ribbon ghosts and layered globe lattice; synthesized audio only. Static Three.js 0.170.0 build, no services. Dev-only state/teleport/currency/FPS bridge is injected only by the dev server. A three-minute headless low-quality playthrough will measure first action, frame rate, purchases, destruction and errors; screenshots at load, 30s, 180s and first major purchase will be compared against the source palette and composition.
