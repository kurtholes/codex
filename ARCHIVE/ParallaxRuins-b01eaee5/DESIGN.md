# Parallax Ruins
Inspiration image: b01eaee5

## Fantasy and verb
Become a little rust-cloaked mapmaker inside an enormous inked parchment. Push a monumental wooden cross, climb its grain, and close one eye to turn fractured checker paths into temporary solid bridges. Weight, scraping, dust and golden light communicate the rules.

## Genre and loop
Third-person exploration puzzler with free orbit, tactile destruction and a persistent tool garden. Explore a landmark → reshape a route → claim its seal → improve a tool → reach farther.

## First ten seconds
Already controllable beside the giant cross; a floating hand marks its hinge. E tips the timber into a walkable causeway with a shower of splinters. No modal tutorial. A stripe of checker fragments beyond it responds to holding Q while looking along the route. Space vaults; R restores nearby structures instantly.

## World and progression
Five physical destinations: Cross Landing, Striped Cathedral, Checker Gardens, Hollow Cliffs, Bare Tree. Seals are earned at renewable route beacons and by breaking brittle ink walls. Six interlinked branches with six tiers each: leverage (push reach and impulse), sight (bridge range and tolerance), stride (speed and dash), grip (jump/climb height), tether (air pull range), resonance (seal yield and aura). Each tier adds luminous copper bands or trailing motes and stronger action effects; higher grip and tether unlock elevated beacons. Costs grow slowly; no prestige reset. Distinct elevated routes reward revisiting the same map.

## Quick play
A seeded shuffled three-beacon course; every movement tool supplied, unlimited rewind, a three-minute timer, medal based on completion time. Restart shuffles the beacon order. Main progress remains separate.

## Why continue
Minute 1: tip a huge detailed cross, leap the chasm and shatter a brittle wall. Minute 10: sight bridges, dash and tether chain together; choose from 36 tool upgrades. Minute 30: reach upper cliff balconies and the tree island, master renewable seal circuits and maximize a visibly embellished traveler.

## Presentation and performance
Ochre mineral terrain, black stipple grain, fibrous timber, alternating broken tiles, rust-red cloth. World-coordinate triplanar procedural surface shader. Instanced cliff striations, debris and dust; fixed debris pool; quality scales pixels/shadows. Synthesized scrape, impact and seal arpeggios. Small icon-led HUD, free roaming camera, no narrative panels. Local saves only.
