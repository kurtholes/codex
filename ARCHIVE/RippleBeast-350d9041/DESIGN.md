# Ripple Beast
Inspiration image: 350d9041

A one-page design, written before implementation.

**Fantasy / verb.** Be the small amber-eyed animal beneath black teal water. Swim with slippery momentum, wrap your entire body around drowned timber, and wrench it apart. An ancient silver-haired hand touches the surface, then points across the lake toward its root-bound owner. Encounters, light and movement carry this relationship.

**Genre / camera.** Open-water creature action and destruction with an orbiting close third-person camera, continuous diving, and a freely traversable drowned forest. Golden eyes and ripples concentrate light against blue-green silhouettes.

**Loop.** Swim → hunt luminous shoals → coil and smash timber/stone → feed → evolve → reach deeper giant-bound roots.

**Play.** Start already swimming beside the hand. Its fingertip moves toward the nearest rotten trunk; gold motes and a tightening body demonstrate holding Space. A trunk fractures into shoveable floating debris. Mouse drags orbit, WASD swim, Q/E change depth, Shift bursts. Every bite and break produces positional ripples, fragments, rising nutrient numbers, synthesized sound and camera kick. No mandatory menus.

**Progression.** Four six-tier anatomical branches: muscle (damage, longer coil, horns), hide (health, armor plates, impact mass), senses (feeding radius, glowing whiskers, sonar), symbiosis (passive feeding, luminous dorsal colonies). Increasing costs and linked prerequisites make specialization matter; ranks 2/4/6 provide silhouette changes. Muscle 2 opens mud channels, hide 2 bank hauling, symbiosis 2 waterfall ascent. Three concentric groves culminate in the giant's root-bound face; breaking its six root bonds frees it. Feeding beds persist and earn nutrients while roaming. Saves preserve anatomy, currency, destruction and records without prestige resets.

**Quick play.** Seeded flood channel, one life, advancing lethal flood, branching barriers and rideable currents. Distance and destruction records persist; one-key restart.

**Minute 1 / 10 / 30.** First minute: fluid swimming, curious hand, first violent trunk snap. Ten: evolved silhouette, stronger stone breaks, new traversal routes, cultivated food. Thirty: specialized six-tier body, deep root destruction and the giant waking; continue growing all branches and chasing flood records.

**Budget.** Pinned Three.js 0.170.0 only; procedural geometry, world-space triplanar surface shader, instanced reeds, fish and debris. Low/medium/high adaptive resolution. Source and static dist; development hooks excluded by build. Verify with a real three-minute headless keyboard playthrough, screenshots and recorded performance, reporting limitations honestly.
