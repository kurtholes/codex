# The Carbs of Destiny
Inspiration image: d397f2e8

Fantasy: an antler-crowned traveler wades through scarlet floodwater under an impossible golden sourdough. Living dough arms hook architecture and wrench it apart. Core verb: grapple and yank.

Genre: third-person free-roaming demolition brawler. The camera follows close behind the scavenger; the city is the playground, the loaf a reachable landmark.

Loop: hook a floor → build elastic tension → yank → cascade nearby structures → absorb grain → mutate → tackle taller districts.

Start already in the waterfront, facing a cracked tower. Click hooks; right click or E yanks. Visible cable tension and a target bracket teach the interaction. No introductory screen. WASD moves, Space jumps, Shift slingshots; buttons offer the mutation tree and instant Collapse Run.

Progression: four interlinked six-tier branches: power (stronger pulls, twin anchors, chain shock), elasticity (reach, slingshot, wall lift), culture (colonies, passive grain, magnetic harvest), crust (armor, raft buoyancy, larger gauntlets). Prices grow geometrically, and every rank changes arm size, glow, reach or movement. Rank thresholds open stronger demolition potential in the antenna forest and root ascent. Colonies turn demolished blocks into sustained income. Loaf roots provide a rising traversal path and breakable crust chunks.

Minute 1: the first tower bends and bursts into slabs; grain streaks home, water rings spread and neighboring floors buckle. Minute 10: longer cables, multi-anchor cascades and slingshots accelerate clearing; cultures reward returning to old blocks. Minute 30: the four branches combine into a powerful mobile scavenger that can demolish the antenna district and climb beneath the loaf.

Quick-play: a deterministic compact Collapse Run, 90 seconds and one life, central tower objective, immediate retry. Main save remains separate.

Feel: synthesized low impacts and elastic twangs, cable sag, camera kick, hit flashes, short hit-stop, rising combo numbers, instanced flying rubble and expanding water shocks. Scarlet fog, black architecture, red sun and luminous detailed crust preserve the reference composition. Procedural world-space triplanar surface noise avoids stretched mapping. Fixed pool sizes and quality presets constrain cost.

Scope: arcade structural physics with floor bodies and neighbor propagation rather than a general rigid-body solver. A large continuous district with reachable roots rather than a full simulated megacity.
