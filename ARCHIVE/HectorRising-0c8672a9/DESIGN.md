# Hector Rising
Inspiration image: 0c8672a9

A stolen skyguard launches from a rope bridge into a towering timber sea fortress. The core verb is **soar and smash**: throw slow blazing cannonballs into supports, fly through the splinters, and vacuum up salvage. Open-world aerial destruction is the best fit for the image's verticality, exposed structural skeleton, bridges and sunset silhouettes.

**Loop:** raid districts → shatter supports and collect salvage → upgrade flight and siege cannon → raid taller offshore bastions.

The opening bridge aims directly at Hector's banner tower. Mouse capture and firing begin on one click; WASD, thrust and cannon are available immediately. Fireballs arc visibly; timber flashes, splinters, falls and splashes. Destroying a platform's supports drops the structure above it. Nearby salvage streams toward the flyer. Small icon prompts teach movement without interrupting play.

Six permanent upgrade branches each have eight tiers: siege mass (larger glowing orbs), penetration (punch through structures), chain fire (secondary blasts), cooling (faster fire), flight (hover, dash, higher altitude) and salvage (wider pickup, orbiting drones). Prices climb gradually; the first purchase takes about thirty seconds. Weapon rings, exhaust trails, larger projectiles and drone count show growth. Outer forts are progressively taller and tougher. No prestige reset. Free roam reaches every district from the beginning; flight makes travel much faster.

Minute 1: the first tower bursts, salvage rushes in, a first upgrade is affordable. Minute 10: chain reactions and sustained flight turn clustered huts into large demolition combos. Minute 30: a fully upgraded skyguard cruises the outer bastions, attacking fresh reinforced structures and chasing high scores. Destroyed districts rebuild after a delay to sustain play.

Quick play starts with a strong loadout and a five-minute clock, escalating destruction score and instant restart. Main progress saves locally. Optional visual story uses the crest, ruined defenses and a distant rival flyer; no dialogue or lore gates.

Palette and silhouette: violet atmospheric sky, molten orange horizon, black-brown lashed timber, steep thatch, rope bridges, gold HECTOR banner. Procedural geometry supplies carved braces, roof layers, posts, ropes and crest. World-position wood grain avoids stretched UVs. Instanced debris, modest shadowless lighting and quality presets aim at integrated graphics.
