# Replaceable procedural assets
All current geometry, sky, water, wood grain, lettering and sound are generated locally at runtime. No external art or audio is required.

| Asset | Generator prompt | Target specification |
|---|---|---|
| Hector tower | Weathered ocean stilt fortress, diagonal timber braces, rope lashings, enormous torn HECTOR banner, gold crest; black timber silhouette | GLB, <25k triangles, 3 LODs, separate supports/platform/banner, meters |
| Thatched outpost | Octagonal timber hut on tall stilts, ragged thatch, rope rails, sunset silhouette | GLB, <8k triangles, breakable roof/walls/supports |
| Skyguard cannon | Stolen bronze naval siege gauntlet, glowing amber core, rope grip, scorched iron rings | GLB, <6k triangles, muzzle at local -Z |
| Timber material | Tar-dark salt-weathered timber, coarse parallel grain and rope scuffs | Seamless 1024² albedo/normal/roughness, triplanar compatible |
| Cannon blast | Heavy low naval cannon thump with magical ember crackle | Mono WAV, 0.6s, normalized -3dB |
| Collapse/splash | Cracking wooden supports then falling debris and deep seawater splash | Mono WAV, 2s, three variations |
| Sea atmosphere | Wind through rope rigging, distant gulls, gentle sea beneath wooden fort | Stereo OGG, seamless 60s, quiet |
| Skyguard rival | Small winged bronze flyer, ember jet, dark silhouette | GLB <4k triangles, simple flight animation |
