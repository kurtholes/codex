# The Infinite Librarian
Inspiration image: bdb16bea

## Fantasy and verb
Be the small, brass-masked keeper of an impossibly tall walnut library. Scoop runaway books into a trolley, carve around balcony rails, and fling entire loads into glowing heraldic shelves. The central hanging lantern is a detailed navigational anchor, surrounded by stacked galleries and warm smoky depth.

## Genre and loop
Third-person vertical traversal and physics-driven management sandbox.
Collect chaotic arrivals → fling bulk loads → earn catalog credits → upgrade trolley, movement and clockwork sorting → explore higher galleries.

## Immediate play
Already moving within one keypress. A book eruption lands ahead of the player; colored shelf symbols pulse at the bridge ends. Movement collects automatically. Click/Space flings the whole trolley toward the nearest matching shelf; arcs and matching colors communicate delivery. Shift grinds rails; E rides ladders between galleries. R shoves bookcases into cascading wreckage and ramps.

## Long progression
Three interconnected eight-tier branches, with rising prices and permanent saves. Trolley increases capacity, magnet radius, value and adds visible brass hoops and book stacks. Traversal adds speed, rail acceleration, ladder vault, double jump and chain flight, opening basement, hanging archive and inverted gallery. Automation adds clockwork arms, conveyor routing, lifts and increasingly frequent passive sorting. Every tier changes equipment or world machinery; no resets. Milestones at tiers 2/4/6 unlock movement and floors. Investment across branches lets a large trolley supply a fast autonomous network.

## Modes and feel
Main mode is an uninterrupted persistent sandbox. One-shift quick-play is a fresh 180-second survival score chase with escalating book storms and overflow pressure; purchases are immediate and its save is isolated. Every scoop makes a note, trail and pulse. Throws create curved swarms, gold numbers, ringing chords and camera kick. Shoves topple cases and scatter instanced debris, which rebuild after a delay. Geometry supports free balcony and bridge travel with forgiving fall recovery.

## Minute 1 / 10 / 30
Minute 1: steer through colorful domino bursts, fling a first satisfying load, buy a visible upgrade. Minute 10: grind between floors with expanded capacity, buy sorting machines, combine shoves and collection. Minute 30: a tall automated operation supports exploration and stronger mass deliveries; maxing all 24 expensive tiers remains a long-term goal.

## Rendering and validation
Pinned Three.js 0.170.0, procedural geometry and world-space triplanar wood grain. Instanced shelf books, loose books and debris. Quality presets adjust pixel ratio, fog and particles. Synthesized audio and localStorage only. Dev build exposes test controls; production strips them. Headless three-minute playthrough records screenshots, frame rate and errors. Scope favors fluid arcade approximation over rigid-body simulation.
