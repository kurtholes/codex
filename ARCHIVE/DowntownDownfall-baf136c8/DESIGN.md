# Downtown Downfall
Inspiration image: baf136c8

Fantasy: emerge from the stranger’s alley, light a twin-thruster rig, and surf beside enormous crawling plasma shells as neon towers fold into the wet street. Core verb: **punch through**.

Genre: open-world aerial destruction sandbox. The copper planet, distant saucers, black silhouettes and luminous road fissures are navigational landmarks. No narrative gates: the whole metropolis is physically accessible immediately; stronger flight makes its upper reaches practical.

Loop: fly into a district → shell supports and swarms → vacuum salvage → grow cannon and rig → carve a path deeper downtown.

Start: already in control, street level in an alley facing a fragile orange support. Mouse fires without a menu. WASD, lift and boost are represented by compact HUD hints. A permanent three-minute quick-run button restarts airborne with a powerful rig and dense opposition; campaign saves separately.

Destruction: each tower has stacked structural segments, glowing vulnerable braces, rooftop machinery, neon strips and antennae. Shells physically traverse the scene; impacts remove segments and unsupported floors tumble as instanced debris. Debris hits drones, shockwaves knock nearby structures, glowing salvage streams to the player. Cannon kick, expanding rings, sparks, pitch-varied synthesized booms and floating damage reward every shot.

Progression: six branches, eight tiers each: shell mass (size/damage), bore (penetration), resonance (shockwave/chains), rig (speed/lift/dash), magnet (pickup reach/healing), scrapper (passive income/automated attacks). Prices grow geometrically. Tier milestones visibly enlarge the cannon, add luminous rings and exhaust, turn single explosions into block-clearing waves and increase vertical reach. Flight tiers unlock sustained climb, dash, recoil surfing and stratospheric boost. Three spatial districts—Foundry, Neon Core and Sky Docks—have increasing heights, armor, salvage and drone pressure. No prestige reset.

Minute 1: a support snaps, a tower cascades, salvage races toward the rig; buy the first upgrade. Minute 10: skim rooftops beside giant penetrating shells, chain neighboring towers, choose rig mobility versus cannon power. Minute 30: automated scrappers support high-altitude dock demolition, dense swarms and multiple maxed branches enable district-scale cascades. Quick play compresses this into a score chase with a clean replay button.

Performance: instanced building segments, windows and dynamic debris; pooled particles, capped drone population, distance visibility and low/medium/high pixel-density presets. Procedural world-space triplanar concrete, no downloaded media; pinned Three.js only. Local save, synthesized audio, development hooks removed during static build.
