# Visual review against baf136c8

The four PNGs show initial control, 30 seconds of demolition, the end of the three-minute run, and the first enlarged-cannon upgrade. They were captured at 960 × 540 on LOW in headless Chromium; the green development FPS overlay is absent from production.

Retained: hooded foreground silhouette and twin flight tanks, alley framing and doubled DOWNTOWN sign, long black central avenue, enormous copper planet, scattered saucer silhouettes, dark layered towers, amber structural lights and magenta neon. The upgrade capture shows a physically larger cannon; the action capture shows crawling ringed plasma shells and removed upper floors.

Differences: the reference is photoreal and densely weathered. The game uses a stylized modular skyline, shader grain and sparse windows, with much simpler lighting and street clutter. Ground highlights approximate wetness rather than faithfully reflecting the skyline. Planet surface detail is procedural and graphic. No rain or volumetric clouds. The three-minute frame hides the result overlay to show the skyline; result.png records the score screen separately. These are visible scope/performance compromises, not claims of visual parity.
