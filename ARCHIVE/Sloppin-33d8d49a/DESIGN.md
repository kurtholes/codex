# SLOPPIN
Inspiration image: 33d8d49a

Become the immense charcoal pig: a living earthmover in a silver, rain-heavy cabbage field. **Root** is the core verb: lower your head, plow, then throw everything ahead of you.

Third-person destruction sandbox, low chase camera. Start already in the patch, no intro. WASD moves, mouse drag or arrows steers, Space roots, Shift charges, E tosses; touch equivalents. Hold rooting through food and fences for immediate response. Quick-play is a fully unlocked, resettable farm with a mud-depth control and a three-minute score chase.

**Loop:** sniff food → root through clutter → eat and grow → evolve anatomy → wreck larger farm structures → invest in deliveries.

Four permanent branches, six tiers each: tusks (tree and fence reach), shoulders (damage and masonry), snout (excavation radius, deeper trenches), hide (charge endurance and thorn passage). Every tier changes size, tusks, bristles or mud markings. Milestones unlock belly surfing, explosive lunge and bank scramble; strength opens orchard and piggery routes. Two infrastructure branches, four tiers each, add increasingly frequent edible deliveries. No prestige. Food is both score and spendable feed; growth depends on lifetime food so spending never shrinks the pig. Save anatomy, lifetime growth and food locally.

Minute 1: cabbages pop, fences splinter, mud sprays; your first upgrade arrives quickly. Minute 10: chain wrecks, surf puddles, uproot orchard trees and specialize the pig. Minute 30: max an anatomy build, flatten the hilltop piggery, maintain deliveries and make the largest wallow. Persistent ruts and movable wreckage turn the field into a record of play.

Art: wet black-brown ground, olive leaves, silver storm sky, distant tree silhouettes. Detailed sculptural pig assembled from layered organic geometry, irregular ears, nostrils, cloven feet, curled tail, bristles and tusks. World-space triplanar procedural noise shades large surfaces. Instanced crops, debris and rain; fixed effect pools. Low/medium/high presets and adaptive initial quality. Synthesized impacts and grunts, no external assets beyond pinned Three.js.

Scope: arcade collision and rigid debris approximations rather than a general physics engine. Flood breach expands a puddle and surf zone. The farm is freely traversable; stronger areas reward upgraded damage, without key gates.
