# The Bridgekeeper
Inspiration image: 127d4570

Become the masked keeper whose lantern commands a violent sea. Core verb: release slow, enormous amber bolts that throw towering sea horrors off timber bridges. Third-person storm-survival action best fits the image's precarious footing, lone silhouette and warm light against a cold storm.

**Loop:** patrol → blast monsters and suspended wreckage → attract salvage → grow the lantern → explore farther bridges.

Play begins on the bridge with an approaching horror and an illuminated lantern. Mouse aims, held fire charges automatically, release fires. Ordinary clicks also deliver weighty shots. WASD moves, Space jumps; icon prompts and a nearby threat teach play. A permanent upgrade dock spends salvage with one key. No dialogue or introductory gates.

The bridge network is open from the beginning, with broken shortcuts repaired beneath the keeper by R. Crates, cargo supports and monsters shatter into instanced debris. Cargo falls onto enemies. The sea heaves below, fog hides the horizon, and amber light reflects along wet timber. A detailed hood, segmented mask, glass goggles, ragged coat and swinging lantern preserve the focal silhouette.

Persistent upgrade tree: six branches with eight tiers each: storm shells (damage, projectile size, penetration), chain fire (detonation radius and extra targets), magnet (collection radius and rewards), sentries (automated fire), charge (faster charge and enormous shells), traversal (speed, grapple at tier 2, air dash at tier 4, flight at tier 6). Tier purchases visibly enlarge the lantern, brighten goggles, add orbiting runes and change projectile trails. Outer wreck field, foundry and fortress contain tougher, more lucrative threats. No prestige resets. Spend through a compact icon dock; locked movement icons illuminate as unlocked.

Quick play: a five-minute siege with movement and weapon upgrades, accelerated rewards, escalating waves and a final leviathan. Separate from persistent main progress.

Minute 1: heavy shots stagger monsters into whitewater; salvage streaks home. Minute 10: chained explosions, automated defenses and grappling make the whole bridge network accessible. Minute 30: giant shells and storm flight turn the distant fortresses into a playground while stronger monsters pressure repairs.

Feel budget: synthesized bass impacts, charge hum, footfalls, hit-stop, camera kick, splinters, glowing salvage and large damage numbers. GPU waves and world-space triplanar noise retain slate, violet, green and amber. Instanced rain, debris and monsters with distance culling keep low quality affordable.
