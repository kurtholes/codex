# TODO / TITAN
Inspiration image: f4cf4ff2

A flying demolition worker faces a colossal silver bureaucratic machine in a black cathedral. Its mouth continually spills paper into the arena. The core verb is **punch through**: slow luminous staples bore through stacks, monuments and organs, releasing showers of paper and salvage.

**Genre:** open-arena flight shooter with incremental demolition progression. Start in flight with the mouth directly ahead, paper targets already in range, and the cannon ready. No introductory screen.

**Loop:** hover → launch piercing staples → collapse structures → magnetize seals → improve the rig → fly higher and deeper.

**Space and feel:** charcoal cracked floor, monumental twin pillars, sculpted mechanical face, white eyes, a moving white paper waterfall. Free movement extends from the ground to the eye balconies and the machinery behind the face. Destructible stacks, segmented columns and gears respawn as an endless workload. Every hit gives impact sparks, fluttering shards, bass, camera kick and rising damage numbers. A visible white coat and industrial flight frame ground the player in the image.

**Progression:** six upgrade branches with eight tiers each: staple power/piercing; paperweight blast radius; chain shredding; magnetic salvage drones; flight and dash; passive shredder income. Costs grow gradually without prestige resets. Cannon barrels, engine exhaust, drone count and shot size grow visibly. Flight tier 2 opens eye altitude; tier 4 unlocks rail surfing and rear chamber access. High organ targets yield greater rewards. Salvage remains useful across long sessions. Quick shift uses a powerful loan rig, escalating floods and a three-minute score clock; campaign saves independently.

**Minute 1:** the first staple bursts through a paper bank; fast salvage gives the first upgrade. **Minute 10:** piercing and blast chains topple monuments while the worker dashes between the eyes. **Minute 30:** the upgraded rig shreds whole banks, drones gather seals, passive machines fund further upgrades, and rear machinery offers denser demolition.

**Controls and onboarding:** concise icon-like HUD: WASD, mouse aim/fire, Space rise, Ctrl descend, Shift dash, E upgrades. A persistent crosshair and target highlight teach shooting. Two prominent mode buttons enable immediate switching. No narrative gates.

**Technical budget:** Three.js 0.170.0, instanced sheets and debris, pooled effects, procedural triplanar stone/metal shaders, capped geometry and pixel ratio presets. Local save only. Synthesized audio. Development test API is removed by the build.
