# BASS REVENANT
Inspiration image: dfc51149

## Fantasy and verb
Become the hooded figure on the battered speaker stack. The wet rooftop is your instrument; the crimson silhouettes across the chasm are your opponents. **Charge → release → reflect.** A huge, slow bass wave crosses the skyline, demolishes machinery and draws a sword return. A timed counter sends it back amplified. Sound, concentric cyan rings, cloth silhouettes and collapsing blocks communicate the rules.

## Genre / loop
Free-roaming rooftop destruction shooter with musical timing, without rhythm charts.
**Spot a red shrine → hover across roofs → trade bass volleys → crush the shrine → absorb salvage → evolve your rig.**

## Play in seconds
The first view is already the playable rooftop, behind a shaped hooded revenant and twin speakers. A single Play button enables audio; movement and charging work immediately. Three large control icons identify movement, charge and counter. The nearest samurai occupies a wide roof directly ahead. Shots aim toward the reticle, with generous close aim assistance. Projectiles are deliberately slow enough to read. Falling recovers onto a roof with a health cost. Captured rigs pay permanent passive salvage.

## Modes
District Run saves upgrades, currency and captures locally. Break through three physical expressway gates by capturing district shrines. Explore the antenna crown, lower flooded roofs and concert fortress beyond. Quick Drop starts a three-minute score attack with flight and a large cannon; instant restart, separate from progression saves.

## Growth
Eight branches, six tiers each: pulse mass (size/damage), wave width (rings/radius), harmonics (pierce), chained echo (extra waves), return gain (counter multiplier/window), hover drive (fuel → sustained flight → air dash → surfing), salvage drones (range/income/orbiting drones), armor rig (health/plating). Costs rise geometrically, gains combine. Every purchased tier changes projectile scale, speaker geometry, aura, drones or movement. District captures provide recurring income, rather than a short prestige reset. Shrines respawn defenders but captured shrine income remains permanent.

## Feel / world
Near-black vertical architecture, cyan wet highlights, dense crimson fog, rain streaks and industrial rooftop silhouettes preserve the source. Detailed procedural hood, skull mask, layered cloak, gauntlets, speakers with recessed cones and hardware. World-position triplanar concrete shader avoids stretched UVs. Breakable rooftop machines and segmented buildings shed instanced debris. Bass synthesized locally; attacks kick camera, hit-stop, scatter particles and show large damage values. Crowds use instanced silhouettes; distant buildings use simple geometry. Low/medium/high select pixel density, rain and debris budgets; automatic conservative hardware selection.

## Minute 1 / 10 / 30
1: Feel the first charged blast smash a roof, learn a counter and earn an immediate upgrade.
10: Surf waves between districts, chain amplified returns and choose mass versus echo investments.
30: A visibly larger speaker rig, orbiting salvage drones, sustained flight, wide multi-wave volleys and fortress demolition. Captured districts fund later tiers while the player pushes higher scores.
