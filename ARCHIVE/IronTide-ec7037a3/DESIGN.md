# IRON TIDE
Inspiration image: ec7037a3

## Fantasy / verb
Captain a soot-black steam tug beneath a monumental iron-crowned queen. TOW: hook, strain, tear, swing, deliver. The image becomes a navigable poisoned shipbreaking harbor, with skeletal ships, rail piers, sulfur fog and cyan foundry mouths.

## Genre / loop
Open-world arcade physics demolition and tug exploration. Cruise → hook machinery → reverse and wrench it free → smash obstacles with the load → deliver to the foundry → permanently improve the tug → breach deeper districts. Start beside a fragile crane; its boom falls through a wreck seam. No introductory screen.

## Play / feedback
WASD steers and drives; Space hooks/releases the nearest highlighted load; Shift overdrives; E banks cargo at the cyan foundry; U opens blueprints. Mouse drag orbits. A visible tension gauge, cable sparks, bending machinery, burst fragments, kick, bass impact and floating salvage values make pulling readable. Quick-play unlocks every rig and offers resetting formations and a timed score chase.

## Long progression
Six interconnected eight-tier branches: torque, cable, hull, grapple, automation, rigs. Costs grow geometrically; engines gain stacks, armor gains ribs, cables glow, paddle wheels appear. Torque 2 supports cable 3; hull 2 supports rigs 3. Rig tiers unlock rail crawling, diving and terrace winching. District seams require progressively greater cable strength. Foundry recovery creates capped offline income; permanent machinery projects multiply deliveries. Equipment profiles trade speed for pulling power. Save locally; no prestige reset.

## Minute 1 / 10 / 30
1: instantly steer, rip the opening crane, scatter sparks and cruise through the broken hull. 10: stronger multi-ton pulls, a visibly rebuilt tug, chained explosions, efficient delivery routes and a second foundry. 30: specialized movement across rails and flooded terraces, high-tier district machinery and long-term foundry projects. Free roam remains available throughout.

## Production constraints
Three.js 0.169.0, pinned jsDelivr module; all art procedural, synthesized audio. World-space triplanar material on large solids, animated oily water, instanced particles/debris. Presets cap resolution. Static dist mirrors source, with development controls excluded. Scope uses lightweight spring/tension and breakable joints rather than a general physics engine.
