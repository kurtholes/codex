# Neon Wilds
Inspiration image: 0d5e7ef1

Become the red-and-white explorer on a wheeled leaf-board in a gigantic midnight garden. Core verb: **carve** — skate curling terrain, sweep shears through orange overgrowth, and carve new lines through the garden.

A third-person open-world traversal playground is the best fit: the image is an invitation to move among impossible botanical silhouettes. A physical mint map with pink pins anchors the space, surrounded by hollow turquoise trunks, vermilion spires, giant flowers and golden stems. Black negative space and etched lines preserve the illustration.

**Loop:** skate → prune for sap → graft stronger equipment → connect pins and ride new rails → reach higher garden districts.

Start already on the board, facing breakable shoots and a golden downhill ribbon. WASD steers, Space launches, mouse/Shift sweeps shears, E rides a nearby rail, and U opens the graft bench. Every cut makes a flash, a short freeze, scattered petals, a woody synthesized snap, currency and a floating number. Broken spires fall into low bridges. Rails and launch leaves let players improvise routes; all visible districts are physically accessible with enough movement skill.

Persistent branching graft tree: wheels (8 tiers: acceleration, speed, trail intensity); shears (8 tiers: sweep radius, chain cutting, larger debris); spring soles (6 tiers: launch height and air control); living infrastructure (6 tiers: rail magnetism and passive tending). Milestones unlock wall carving, stem grapple and catapult launches. Bought tiers visibly enlarge the board, blade arc, neon trails and planted traversal leaves. No resets. Main progression is saved locally. Quick-play starts immediately with powerful equipment and a 90-second downhill score chase, instant R retry.

Minute 1: responsive movement, first cascade of flying petals, first cheap upgrade. Minute 10: chaining pin rails, spring leaves, cuts and jumps while choosing specialized grafts. Minute 30: fully luminous board, wide chain sweeps, high flower crowns and aerial routes, self-tending sap infrastructure and player-made shortcuts.

Rendering: detailed procedural curled leaves, hollow trunks, petal meshes, etched botanical veins, sculpted coat explorer; world-space triplanar grain on major surfaces. Instanced debris and shoots, bounded particles, distance visibility, adjustable pixel ratio. Synthesized audio, local saves, no runtime service calls beyond the pinned Three.js module download. Development hooks live only in the source entry; production build removes them.
