# The Many Paths
Inspiration image: 01e1599d

Become the silhouetted traveler in the mirror sea, but move with the speed of lightning. **Sprint, leap, grind, slam.** Third-person open-world traversal playground: the horizon is a destination, and the line behind you is something you made.

Loop: chase a landmark → carve a persistent light route → shatter crystals → bank at a crossroads → grow your movement and route network.

Start in motion. A luminous starting ribbon bends through fragile crystal fans, then into the first banking halo. Space and a downward arrow appear as compact visual hints. Steering creates the route; all visible mountains, moon shores and ruins can be approached. Speed builds on the ground, jumps preserve it, slams convert height into destruction. Blue branching currents reward precise steering. Routes carry moving ghost travelers and generate passive energy.

Progression: six connected constellation branches, each with eight increasingly expensive tiers: Stride (speed and longer gold wake), Wings (jump height, double jump, aerial steering), Impact (slam radius, debris bloom, shock rings), Current (route income and ghost density), Skipper (boost efficiency, wave skipping and luminous footfalls), Tether (air dash, extended reach, moonward lift). Milestones unlock elevated cloud ridges, sunken ruin crowns and low-gravity moon platforms. Each purchase changes trail color/width, runner aura or movement. Long investments, no prestige reset.

Quick Crossing: a fresh seeded branching ribbon above a void, one life, 90 seconds, three finish gates. Hard outer gates pay more. Falling ends the run; immediate restart. Main-world progression remains separate.

Minute 1: movement immediately feels good; crystals explode, huge energy scores rise, first bank buys an upgrade. Minute 10: routes form a living network and movement combinations reach new heights. Minute 30: powerful jumps, wide impact wakes and ghost traffic turn the mirror sea into a personal constellation.

Mood: navy and lavender, peach-gold sunrise, jagged mountains in mist, huge crescent worlds, white-blue and gold ribbons reflected in the ocean. Procedural sculpted cloak runner, layered rocky shores, crystal fans and broken arches. Terrain uses world-space triplanar procedural grain. Instanced obstacles, debris and ghost traffic; capped route geometry and quality presets keep the toy light.
