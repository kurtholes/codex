# AIR / A home against the cold
Inspiration image: 22ee0ab5

Fantasy: inhabit the scratched, frost-crusted visor in the photograph. Weighty boots cross a blue-gray alien waste; a tiny amber greenhouse is the warm center of a gradually habitable world. Core verb: cut salvage, then turn it into breathing room.

Genre: first-person open-world survival homesteading, with light automation and persistent equipment progression. No dialogue, keys, or puzzle gates. The world starts moving immediately.

Loop: cut wreckage and harvest luminous flora → retreat into warm air → cultivate and upgrade → cross farther, harsher terrain.

A leaking supply crate is directly in front of the player. Hold mouse to fracture its armored shell. Fragments scatter, salvage numbers rise, air falls, and a glowing home beacon stays visible. E feeds algae at the greenhouse; Q opens compact upgrade branches. Every tier changes a tool, tank, garden, or movement capability. Six branches, six tiers each: cutter output/reach; scrubber capacity/refill; insulation/air economy; traction/sprint/winch/jets/rover; algae strains/bed yield; automation/drones/pipelines/atmospheric garden. Costs rise over 36 lasting purchases, with no resets. Distant wreck fields, a ravine, geothermal basin, and plateau reward longer excursions and mobility. No invisible walls; terrain elevation and distance impose practical limits.

Minute 1: crack a crate, see debris and glowing resources fly, retreat to a shelter and plant first algae. Minute 10: upgraded cutter cleaves several cargo shells; beds produce salvage, larger tanks support distant exploration, jets change traversal. Minute 30: automated greenhouse, linked air stations, fast rover movement and high-tier wrecks create a broad harvesting circuit and a visibly green home.

Quick play: separate ten-minute run, borrowed tier-two gear and a working garden. Recover 400 salvage before the storm, with a persistent best score; main save is untouched.

Feel: procedural crunches, cutter pulses, low oxygen heartbeat, synth wind, spring tool recoil, flying instanced fragments and floating rewards. Blue-gray fog and charcoal machinery contrast amber glass, lime algae and silver frost. Detailed ribbed greenhouse, glass panels, tanks, pipes, bolts, bent wreck hulls, a modeled wrist/tool silhouette, and curved visor border carry the image's industrial shapes. Large surfaces use world-space triplanar procedural grain.

Technical: pinned Three.js 0.170.0; procedural assets and synthesized sound only. Static source copied to dist, build strips development state hooks. Seeded environment; instanced snow/debris; pixel ratio presets; persistent localStorage. Test mode only in source supports teleport/currency/state/FPS. Keyboard/mouse primary.
