# MOONFALL
Inspiration image: ee7b1ff3

## Fantasy / verbs
Run on a cold, heaving ocean of stone beneath impossible crescent moons. The amber peak shelters a crystalline escape craft. RUN, JUMP, LAUNCH, FLY, SHATTER. A tactile survival arcade sandbox, moving immediately on load; no narrative gate.

## Loop
Sprint across tidal ground → launch at the amber sanctuary → dodge gravity wells and pulverize falling moonstone → bank salvage → evolve suit and ship → escape through a higher orbital layer.

## Space and feel
Lavender dusk, peach-lit crescents, reflective ice, black serrated ridges. A faceted, asymmetric amber crown with a fractured core dominates the horizon. The ship has swept fins, twin engines, a crystal canopy and articulated cannon. Free movement across the entire plain; launch anywhere after reaching the ship. Orange impact telegraphs, expanding craters, fractured ice, scatter debris and collectible shards communicate danger and reward. Cannon recoil, synthesized bass, particles, floating numbers and brief hit-stop make every shot physical. Main runs climb orbital layers until hull failure; banked salvage persists. Quick play is a 90-second dash with automatic launch after 25 seconds.

## Long progression
Six branches, eight tiers each; prices grow exponentially and no prestige reset. Suit: sprint and jet height; boots: tide riding and reduced impact damage; shield: hull and recharge; engines: flight speed, wider fins and extra engine rings; magnet: salvage radius and amber satellites; cannon: damage, blast radius, twin barrels at tier 3 and heavy moonbreaker at tier 5. Tier 2 suit grants sustained jetpack; tier 2 engines opens upper orbit, tier 4 opens shattered belt, tier 6 opens lunar wake. Each branch displays purchased tiers and next numeric benefit. Every flight earns a time-based extraction bonus, with chunk destruction the faster source of salvage.

## Minute 1 / 10 / 30
1: run, leap over a tide, reach the bright ship, fire a booming cannon into a fragment and watch it burst into salvage. 10: jetpack across waves, slingshot through wells, choose between mobility and a heavier gun; survive a new altitude. 30: eight-tier branches combine a broad salvage field, massive moonbreaker blasts and fast upper-orbit routes. Longer runs bring denser fragments and higher rewards, without deleting earlier purchases.

## Delivery / budget
Three.js 0.170.0 pinned jsDelivr import, no other runtime dependency. Shared geometry and instanced rubble/particles, capped hazards, adjustable pixel ratio. World-space triplanar procedural terrain shading. Synthesized audio after input. Local save. Separate development entry exposes test controls; production build omits them. Three-minute real-time headless simulation and screenshots document actual results, not an assumed 60 fps.
