# SMOKERING
Inspiration image: 5a1da71e

A one-page design · aerial village defense

**Fantasy / verb.** Be the soot-cloaked keeper of a fragile timber settlement. Fortify, lift off, and lob enormous glowing embers across blue-black rooftops. The central chimney is a beacon and the thing you protect.

**Genre.** Open-layout tower defense with direct aerial arcade combat. The whole village is available immediately; outer clearings unlock through permanent rig investment.

**Loop.** Blast crawlers → automatically bank scrap → build and evolve defenses → fly farther → summon a larger siege.

**Immediate play.** Start standing on the south roof, cannon ready, three amber enemy trails approaching. Mouse aims at the ground; hold fire, WASD moves, Space lifts. Nearby cyan sockets advertise building. No introduction or modal menu. Quick Siege is one click and lasts ten minutes, ending with a giant crawler.

**World.** Concentric crooked walkways, thirty pointed huts, leaning palisades and a tall pale smoke column in dense teal woodland. Detailed roof shingles, beams, stilts and empty doorways preserve the reference silhouette. Timber breaks into pooled chunky debris. Broken foundations remain buildable; enemies slow around surviving buildings and cross gaps more slowly.

**Progression.** Six permanent branches, eight tiers each: ember damage/size/piercing; shell splitting; flight duration/height/air dash; tower fire rate/linking; repair/core armor; expansion/scrap yield. Costs grow geometrically. Tier three is the first major upgrade: twin exhaust wings, oversized cannon, canopy access. Tier six adds flooded hollow access and sustained flight. Four tower types each gain levels: harpoon, mortar, smoke vent, collector. Buildings can be restored on their foundations. Wave rewards and surviving collectors drive continued growth. No prestige reset. Save purchases and best wave locally.

**Minute 1.** Huge slow shots explode into legs and timber; flight makes the circular layout immediately readable. **Minute 10.** Interlocking mortars and vents hold two approaches while the player handles giants; branching purchases change shots and mobility. **Minute 30.** High-tier split siege shells and linked fire cover an expanded village; rebuilding, repairs and positioning keep power useful.

**Feel / performance.** Synthesized low cannon thumps, wood snaps, rising reward tones; recoil, impact flash, damage numerals, smoke and pooled instanced debris. Fixed population budgets and quality presets target integrated GPUs. A wide third-person camera preserves orientation, with aim assistance for readable flying combat.
