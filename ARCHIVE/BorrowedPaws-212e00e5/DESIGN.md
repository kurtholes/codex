# Borrowed Paws
Inspiration image: 212e00e5

## Fantasy and verb
Be the amber guardian beside a small red-haired kid in a sunlit, mint-hazed neighborhood. **Borrow a body, make his afternoon better.** Instant possession sends golden filaments between a shaggy dog, swallow and neighbor; each body has a distinct silhouette, camera height, movement and physical action.

## Genre and first ten seconds
Third-person possession action sandbox. Start moving as the dog immediately: a cart rolls toward the kid, a large paw marker invites a body-check. No intro or dialogue. The kid celebrates, then runs to his ball. Golden trails and pictorial markers lead onward, with no forced route.

## Loop
Spot the kid's interest → possess a useful host → sprint, fetch, smash, fly or build → earn sunshine → strengthen your guardian constellation → reach new play spaces.

## World and feedback
Golden grass, deep shadow, mint sky and teal city silhouettes surround a detailed russet dog and red-shirted child. A translucent amber guardian hovers behind possessed bodies. Fences and crates scatter into pooled instanced debris. Impacts use camera kick, flash, particles, synthesized sound, floating reward numbers and brief hit-stop. Terrain and architecture use world-space triplanar procedural color. Free roam connects meadow, gardens, workshop and riverside, with elevated roofs accessible by bird. Activities repeat to support extended play.

## Progression
Four upgrade branches, six tiers each, prices rising gradually without resets. Paws: speed, ram force, jump height, wall scramble, golden fur, long leaps. Wings: flight speed, lift, carry radius, dive power, gilded feathers, sky dash. Hands: stride, strength, build radius, workshop access, golden sleeves, master builder. Halo: possession reach, reward multiplier, longer trails, riverside access, orbiting lights, companion commands. All tiers strengthen stats and change halo scale/trails; milestones add specific movements and garden/workshop/river shortcuts. Unlocks supplement free exploration rather than invisible gates. Kid confidence and completed activities persist with currency and upgrades.

## Modes and staying power
Main mode saves a long-lived neighborhood. Quick-play starts a fresh three-minute afternoon with three shuffled activity wishes and separate score, without changing the main save. Minute 1: rescue a cart, fetch and kick a ball, leap between bodies. Minute 10: chain dog charges, bird dives and human repairs while upgrading all branches. Minute 30: traversal becomes extravagant, rooftops and riverside connect, companions help, and stronger impacts yield larger showers of sunshine. The toy is movement and physical improvisation; wishes provide direction without forcing a sequence.
