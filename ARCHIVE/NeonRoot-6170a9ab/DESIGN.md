# NEON ROOT
Inspiration image: 6170a9ab

Become the obsidian spellcaster: sculpted black anatomy, wild ivory hair, a living furnace for a heart, and a gold-eyed black cat. Core verb: hurl heavy crawling heartfire, then plant roots to drink the scorched earth.

Genre: free-roaming destruction action / horde defense. The player starts in the world immediately. A nearby brittle crystal teaches shooting by breaking spectacularly; pursuing guardians teach movement; healing grows visible roots. No dialogue gates.

Loop: roam → shatter growths and guardians → vacuum embers → root and recover → evolve → reach richer terrain.

The midnight violet grove surrounds a pink striped sun. Crystal basins stretch east, tall ruins north, and floating fragments overhead. Everything visible is reachable; travel upgrades make height and distance easier. Destructible crystal clusters and segmented towers burst into pooled instanced fragments. Guardians keep pressure on an otherwise meditative ruin field.

Permanent evolution has eight branches, six tiers each: heart mass (size/damage), furnace (cadence), piercing (penetration), chain roots (area eruptions), deep roots (healing/range), nutrient reservoir (health), familiar (collection/rate), and ascension (speed → leap → grapple impulse → flight). Costs rise geometrically; first evolution costs 35 embers. Body glow, crown, orbiting fire and movement change with investment. No prestige resets. Regions award increasing rewards and tougher guardians. Quick hunt equips flight and a large cannon for five minutes, banks embers, and returns to the persistent body.

Minute 1: slow bright projectiles explode purple spires, numbers pop, the cat gathers sparks, rooting restores health. Minute 10: several branches combine into larger piercing shots and healing shockwaves; leaps carry the caster onto ruins. Minute 30: layered damage and cat gathering sustain hovering bombardment in remote crystal fields; many expensive tiers remain.

Feel budget: shot recoil, bass thump, impact freeze, camera shake, orange shock rings, hundreds of cheap fragments, floating damage and ember trails. Synthesized audio starts on first input. UI is a small instrument panel, icons and short labels; upgrades pause combat. Keyboard/mouse first, optional touch controls.

Technical: Three.js 0.170.0 pinned jsDelivr module; no other runtime libraries. Procedural meshes, world-space triplanar shader on ground/ruins, instanced debris and guardians, capped effects, quality presets. localStorage progression. Source dev hooks are removed by build. Automated three-minute low-quality browser run records first action, FPS, errors and four screenshots.
