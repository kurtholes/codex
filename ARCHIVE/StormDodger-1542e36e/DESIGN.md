# Storm Dodger
Inspiration image: 1542e36e

You are the white-shirted matador beneath a living wall of weather. Your verb is DODGE: hold the bull's attention, commit to a last-second sidestep, and let its momentum demolish the frontier behind you. Third-person open-plain destruction arcade best fits the image's scale, isolation, and looming horns.

**Loop:** lure → read the glowing charge lane → dodge → shatter a ranch → bank Olé → improve your kit → challenge larger weather.

The game starts live, with a bull facing the player and a breakable ranch directly behind them. WASD movement, Space dodge, E cape taunt; icons and a ground lane teach timing. A persistent workshop opens with Tab. One Charge is an independent two-minute score attack. Free roaming connects Farm Valley, Rail Town, Oil Field and Canyon; no collectible gates.

**Progression:** six branches, eight tiers each: Boots (longer dodge, slide, air step), Cape (wider lure, red-gold ribbons), Nerve (larger perfect window, healing), Fortune (score multiplier), Weather (larger bulls, twins, ember/hail), and Rider (vortex launch, glide, storm riding). Costs grow across 48 purchases. Every branch changes mechanics; garment color, trails, bull scale and orbiting debris express growth. Regions have denser, larger destructibles and reward multipliers. No prestige reset. Late Rider lets the player mount and steer a storm through buildings.

**Minute 1:** an enormous sculpted cloud-bull lowers ivory horns, a warning lane burns gold, and one dodge sends a barn flying. Perfect timing produces slow motion, bass impact, cape snap and a large score burst.
**Minute 10:** upgraded movement links close dodges across multiple bulls while the player steers charges through town blocks and stacks larger destruction bonuses.
**Minute 30:** high-tier lures and storm riding turn oil derricks and canyon settlements into airborne debris; the player chooses danger and builds toward all 48 tiers.

**Art / performance:** charcoal atmosphere, luminous cream horizon, ochre prairie, tiny white shirt and scarlet cape. Bull silhouette uses layered irregular cloud lobes, a heavy muzzle, lowered brow, curved tapered horns and ember eyes. Procedural world-space triplanar terrain and structures; instanced grass, clouds and debris; bounded debris pool; three quality settings and automatic initial selection. Synthesized wind, rumbles and cape sounds. Save purchases locally. Tests use a development-only state hook excluded by the static build script.
