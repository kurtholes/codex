# Blackwake Reach
Inspiration image: a107ed12

## One-page design
Fantasy: steer the enormous black-sailed vessel out of a silver-fog canyon. The core verb is **sail**: carve around rocks, bring a broadside to bear, and blast a path to any visible shoreline.

Genre: open-world pirate action adventure with arcade naval combat and compact third-person shore raids. Begin already moving, with an enemy crossing the bow. No opening modal.

Loop: spot lanterns → sail into danger → broadside ships and forts → claim wreck cargo and harbors → refit → reach farther water.

The toy is momentum plus destruction. Hold fire to throw alternating broadsides, watch individual planks scatter, then skim the floating wreck for gold. A grapnel pulls the ship sideways. At beaches and docks, step ashore as a coat-clad captain, slash defenders and shatter crates; the anchored ship stays visible. Harbors produce supplies over time.

Permanent blueprint branches, six tiers each: hull (armor and more ornate ribs), sail (speed, extra canvas), battery (more barrels and heavier volleys), rigging (turn speed, grapnel reach), boarding (cutlass damage and pistol), empire (harbor income). Costs rise substantially; fittings change geometry and feel. Milestones unlock shallow water, timber gate ramming, cliff grapnel routes and the outer wreck field. No prestige. Ship refit is available while roaming, using earned cargo.

Quick play: Harbor Breakout starts instantly with one hull, randomized batteries and a marked escape beyond the inlet. Survive and cross the boundary for a result; restart is immediate.

Minute 1: steer and fire in seconds, first splintering enemy and cargo pickup. Minute 10: stronger silhouettes, several captured islands, shore skirmishes and route choices. Minute 30: upgraded broadside fleet-killer, income network and distant heavy fort targets.

Art: layered silver fog, slate water with moving pale foam, huge dark angular cliffs, nearly black curved sails, detailed ribbed wooden hull and sparse amber lamps. Triplanar procedural grain on rock and timber. Camera roll, hit pause, recoil, sparks, debris and synthesized bass cannon reports sell weight.

Scope: a continuous procedural archipelago with compact island raids, destructible fort sections and ships; abstract crew and economy. Desktop keyboard/mouse first. Three quality presets cap debris and pixel ratio. Persistent upgrades and harbor ownership; quick play is isolated.
