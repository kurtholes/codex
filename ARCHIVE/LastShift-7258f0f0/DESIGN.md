# Last Shift
Inspiration image: 7258f0f0

## Fantasy and verb
A chipped ivory office robot keeps working in a drowned Gothic city. **Trudge, winch, bulldoze, stamp.** Amber eyes, chest-deep teal water, impossible columns, and a cyan rejected coversheet make its tiny persistence tangible.

## Form
Third-person open-world delivery playground. Close trailing camera, freely navigable cathedral, archive courtyard and industrial quay. No dialogue or narrative gates. The first coversheet is visible on arrival behind floating cabinets: grab a cabinet with the winch, move it, retrieve the sheet, return to the amber inbox.

## Loop
Winch a marked parcel → haul through breakable clutter → stamp at the inbox → spend wages on a visibly stronger chassis → haul bigger jobs farther away.

## Progression
Six branches with six permanent tiers each: torque (hauling force, larger motors), cable (reach and luminous spool), chassis (speed and plated shoulders), hydraulics (boost and leg extensions), salvage (impact rewards and amber salvage coils), logistics (wages and courier beacons). Costs scale over a long save. Tier thresholds unlock heavier cargo and district contracts. Boost, stronger winch and destructible barriers create new routes; all districts can be explored immediately. First major upgrade adds visible hardware and a blue exhaust trail. Workshop earnings accumulate while playing; progression never resets. The implementation concentrates on an interlinked yard rather than a simulated city.

## Quick play
One Shift, One Parcel: seeded cargo position, supplied equipment, 90 seconds, cargo condition reduces payout, one attempt per run. Independent of the career save. Instant mode switch.

## Feel
Foot splashes and low mechanical ticks; taut cyan cable; buoyant tilting cabinets; heavy shove resistance; breakable crates and masonry with instanced debris; impact flash, camera kick, sparks, synthesized crunch, and floating wage numbers. Paper delivery produces a giant red stamp and a warm chord.

## Minute 1 / 10 / 30
1: feel water resistance, pull a cabinet, deliver the missing sheet, buy a visible motor. 10: smash routes, chain larger deliveries, branch upgrades and use boost to cross the yard quickly. 30: powerful persistent machine with upgraded handling, higher paying heavy cargo and route familiarity. Long progression lives in the six-branch workshop; geography remains compact.

## Technical targets
Three.js 0.170.0 only, procedural geometry and shader world-space triplanar stone, synthesized sound, localStorage career. Instanced rubble and windows, capped particles, low/medium/high presets. Production build strips development state hooks. Automated Chromium run measures first action, low preset FPS, errors and timed screenshots.
