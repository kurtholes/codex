# Glowball Grove
Inspiration image: 8367c6d5

A tiny copper-winged gardener beetle pushes a sun-sized pollen ball through cobalt grass. The core verb is **roll**: steer weight, bank down slopes, vacuum fireflies, and shatter dry garden timber. The genre is a third-person physics playground with persistent garden production. The cottage, golden trail, mint tree, low moon and violet wetlands are actual destinations in one continuous valley.

**Loop:** Gather glow → enlarge your ball → smash timber → plant groves → harvest passive glow → evolve → roll farther.

Start already playing, with a glowing trail flowing toward a brittle log. WASD moves, mouse drag or arrows turns the view, Shift surges, Space hops, E plants, Tab opens botanical research. No intro or blocking tutorial. An icon strip carries the controls. Quick roll resets a 90-second downhill scoring run without affecting the garden save.

Research has six branches and eight tiers each (48 purchases), rising geometric costs and visible changes: pollen volume and satellites; rolling speed and longer wakes; magnetic collection and orbiting lights; garden yield and taller flowering plants; spring legs unlocking high jumps then glide; root shells unlocking the wetland and canopy via climbing and buoyancy. Every branch contributes to the rolling loop. Major evolutions at total levels 4, 12 and 24 change the ball's halo, beetle wings and collection reach. Routes can be explored immediately; abilities make rough terrain easier. No prestige resets.

The garden stores locations and tiers, pays while exploring, and accumulates up to eight hours away. Planting spends ball glow and creates a permanent mint-and-gold flowering grove; productive groves finance branching upgrades. Tracks mark traversed ground. Timber respawns slowly to sustain the toy. Destruction scatters pooled instanced splinters with gravity and bouncing, accompanied by camera kick, a brief impact freeze, synthesis and large rising rewards.

Minute 1: learn rolling by following fireflies, smash the first log, plant your first grove. Minute 10: chain downhill surges, evolve several branches, jump across the stream and invest garden yield. Minute 30: develop a luminous forest, reach the giant tree's canopy and tune a fast magnetic ball for repeated quick-run score attempts.

Art: procedural sculpted branching tree, layered translucent mint canopy; individually shaped cottage roof, chimney, window frames and winding stone path; velvet cobalt hills under a shader-painted violet spiral sky. World-space triplanar noise on terrain and large solids. Low chase camera, spring legs and an illuminated pollen sphere establish scale. Instanced grass, fireflies, debris and path stones; capped pixel ratio and preset instance counts keep integrated GPUs viable. Synthesized audio starts on first input.

Verification: production build removes the development bridge. A separate development build exposes teleport, currency, state and FPS. Headless keyboard-driven three-minute session captures 0s, 30s, 180s and major evolution; report actual rendering FPS and errors, with limitations called out.
