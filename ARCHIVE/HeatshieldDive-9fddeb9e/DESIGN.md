# HEATSHIELD / DIVE
Inspiration image: 9fddeb9e

## Fantasy & verb
The station is coming apart around you. A white EVA suit, a torn ceramic panel under your boots, an impossibly blue Earth below. **SURF** the debris through re-entry; **TREK** across your landing island to the rescue transmitter. The camera rides behind the astronaut, with the exploding station occupying the horizon. No introduction: descent starts immediately.

## Genre & loop
A momentum-driven re-entry arcade game flowing into a small open-world survival trek. Quick play compresses the dive into sixty seconds with a score chase.

**Carve through salvage → skim wrecks for multipliers → brake against heat → land → reach a signal tower → invest salvage → launch into a harder orbit.**

## Physical play
WASD steers the fragment. Shift spends air on boost; Space pitches the shield into a heat-shedding brake, then deploys a chute below the cloud deck. Nearby shattered modules shower collectible metal; collisions break panels and trade suit integrity for salvage. Fast near misses reward brave lines. Air and heat exert opposite pressure: slow is cool, fast preserves air. Landing converts the same controls into walking, sprinting and jet-assisted jumps. An island contains freely reachable ridges, scattered caches, breakable supply crates and a visible rescue mast. E at the mast banks a rescue bonus and relaunches. U opens the upgrade bench without ending the session.

## Long progression
Six branches, each six tiers (36 purchases), escalating costs and persistent saves: ceramic shield (thermal limit, enlarged glowing hexagon layers); oxygen (tank capacity, paired tank geometry); jets (lateral acceleration, increasingly bright exhaust); landing system (chute, wings, safe landing); trek equipment (stride, sprint, jet hops); orbit access (larger rewards, three new orbit/landing-region palettes). Tier 2 jets unlocks strong boost; tier 2 landing adds wings; tier 3 trek adds sustained hops. Orbit tiers move the rescue destination and change the terrain region. Currency comes from play, never a reset. A failed dive still banks earned salvage and instantly offers another attempt.

## Why stay?
Minute 1: steer immediately, hear fragments crack, burn a trail, find the cool angle and burst through clouds. Minute 10: a wider shield, twin tanks and hotter jets make risky salvage lines feel controllable; land closer to the mast and shorten the trek. Minute 30: combine high-tier air, flight and ground gear, unlock rougher landing regions and chase high-score sixty-second runs.

## Presentation & budget
Deep black, cold blue limb light, tiny bright stars, a detailed cylindrical station with exposed rings, ribbed modules, solar wings and a turbulent orange fireball. Procedural world-space triplanar material on large surfaces. Instanced debris, clouds and stars; limited particles and pixel ratio by quality preset. Synthesized impact, boost and wind audio. Diegetic meters and compact icon instructions, no lore cards. Three.js 0.170.0 only runtime dependency; pinned CDN build bundled for offline play. Production excludes the dev state hook. Save locally; no telemetry or remote services.
