# Under the Red Sky
Inspiration image: 195f7cae

Fantasy: start behind the wheel on a cracked highway, the last amber diner glowing beneath a violent red sky. Fire enormous, crawling plasma shells, then chase their destruction into the wilderness. Core verb: bombard.

Genre: open-world driving and aerial destruction sandbox with persistent incremental upgrades. The diner is a safe garage; everything beyond it is a playground.

Loop: roam → bombard swarms and roadside compounds → sweep up scrap → upgrade at the diner → travel farther.

The first frame is playable. A target compound directly ahead invites the first shot; luminous shells, shockwaves, flying chunks and rising scrap numbers teach the action. WASD steers, mouse aims, click fires, Space engages flight, Shift boosts. E near the OPEN sign opens a compact icon-led garage. A five-minute siege button loans advanced flight and a cannon, immediately spawning escalating attacks.

Progression: eight branches, six tiers each: shell mass (larger glowing projectile and recoil); blast (wider shock rings); penetration (extra structures pierced); chain (secondary electrical explosions); salvage drones (orbiting collectors); passive salvage (income while exploring); flight (longer hover, air dash, sustained lift); engine/range (speed, recoil boost, car glider and access to lake, canyon and mountain compounds). Costs compound gently. Milestone tiers visibly add cannon rings, exhaust plumes, drones and wing panels. Long progression persists without resets.

Minute 1: enjoy suspension, shell anticipation, explosive scenery, magnetic salvage and immediate upgrades. Minute 10: combine flight and recoil, chain entire motel rows, use drones and choose upgrade branches. Minute 30: cruise across three regions with a winged cannon car, bigger shells and self-funding salvage; challenge personal siege scores.

Art: black desert silhouettes, scarlet layered procedural clouds, red reflective cracked asphalt, finely shaped diner with roof trim, mullions, booths, counter, door and neon OPEN. Procedural world-space triplanar surfaces avoid stretched texture coordinates. Diner cannot be destroyed. Roadside walls, canopy, signs and swarm creatures react with pooled instanced debris. Sound is synthesized; shot, impact, salvage and engine have distinct envelopes.

Performance: fixed population limits, instanced rocks/debris/swarm, distance visibility and quality presets. Save upgrades and scrap to localStorage. Production excludes the dev state API; development exposes teleport, grants and performance stats for a three-minute browser test.
