# The Sleeping Ones
Inspiration image: 3fbe3bda

## Fantasy and verb
Become the luminous white traveler beneath serenely sleeping, moon-faced giants. Skim rusted tracks, rise into violet fog, and launch huge slow dream shells. Their deliberate approach ends in thunder, buckling steel and a rain of cyan energy. The giants never wake.

## Genre and loop
Open-world aerial destruction playground with long incremental progression.
Explore → shell derelict rail yards and root arches → absorb blue energy → grow flight and artillery → fly toward more distant, stranger silhouettes.

## First ten seconds
The traveler already hovers on railway tracks. A bright shell is loaded; a damaged carriage fills the aiming reticle. Hold mouse to fire, WASD to glide. The first shot breaks carriage panels, draws energy directly into the traveler and reveals the upgrade icon. No blocking introduction.

## Progression
Six branches, eight permanent tiers each, purchased with escalating energy costs: Mass (larger luminous shells, blast radius); Lance (penetration and glowing shell rings); Echo (delayed secondary detonations); Cascade (linked explosions); Magnet (larger absorption field and orbiting energy); Wings (longer dress ribbons, faster flight, rail boost at tier 1, sustained lift at tier 2, air dash at tier 3, high-altitude soaring at tier 4). Two additional eight-tier branches: Halo (orbiting automatic cannons) and Reactor (reload speed, brighter traveler). Each branch has a visible change and numerical benefit. Upgrade costs grow slowly enough to keep the next purchase in reach. Three regions expand vertically and outward: starting yard, flooded station, elevated root city. Travel is unrestricted; flight upgrades make higher targets practical. Destroyed salvage regrows after a long interval to support continued play. Saves persist across reloads. No prestige reset.

## Quick play
Separate five-minute demolition run. Enhanced wings and artillery from the first frame, a live score and best score; every 20 seconds choose one of three free upgrades with number keys. Main progression remains separate.

## Feel and art
Cobalt night, violet horizon, cyan moon faces, sparse stars, layered ground mist, rust rails and luminous white fabric. Giants have sculpted brow ridges, closed eyelids, small lips and twisting open-root trunks. Traveler has a shaped torso, head, arms and many animated trailing cloth ribbons. Slow shells have rings and luminous tails; impacts produce camera kick, brief hit stop, synthesized bass, sparks, flying carriage panels and floating large damage values. Instanced debris and salvage bound draw calls. World-space triplanar procedural grain covers ground and large surfaces.

## Why keep playing
Minute 1: immediate low-flight pleasure, a satisfying first carriage explosion and two affordable purchases.
Minute 10: overlapping shells, chain blasts, automatic satellites and dash change the rhythm; flooded stations offer denser targets.
Minute 30: enormous artillery pulses and long white ribbons sweep elevated rail cities, with many of the 64 upgrade tiers still to buy. The pleasure is mastery of movement and escalating physical spectacle, with no dialogue or required reading.
