# Source comparison
Inspiration image: 9e39585f

The 1280×714 captures use the source image's aspect ratio. The starting view retains two opposing blue-black concrete façades, barred windows, wet teal paving, floating heart silhouettes, a low central orange sun, and a red heart vent. The game changes the source's third-person central figure to a first-person blade and offhand gun. Architecture is more regular, the sky is a procedural flat fog field rather than photographic clouds, and wet reflections are stylized rather than ray traced.

The 30-second and three-minute frames capture active combat rather than recreating the source composition. Upgrade capture shows the first Edge tier and its visibly extended blade. Chrome reflections are generated from a local procedural cube environment. This deliberately avoids shipping the source photograph as a background.
