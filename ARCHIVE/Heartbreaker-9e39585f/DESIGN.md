# HEARTBREAKER
Inspiration image: 9e39585f

Fantasy: move through a wet, blue-black prison district beneath a dying sun, cutting mirrored heart drones apart with a red laser sword and an offhand gun. Core verb: carve.

Genre: first-person free-roaming arcade combat. Mouse-directed sweeps, reflected projectiles, readable incoming formations, destructible façades and a recharge vent make the courtyard a physical playground.

Loop: cut / shoot → absorb cores → improve a permanent branch → breach deeper courtyards → survive stronger formations.

Start already in the world. Click engages the blade and mouse look; WASD moves, right mouse fires, Space dodges. Nearby hearts teach through luminous slash marks. The floor heart replenishes health. Tab exposes a compact upgrade workbench. Quick-play starts a separate one-life score run with a selectable kit and extraction after wave five.

Progression: six linked branches, eight tiers each: Edge (damage, longer blade, execution arcs), Capacitor (faster gun, multi-shot, electrical rounds), Magnet (core yield, attraction), Chassis (health, armor), Vector (dash, slide, wall boost, grapple), Breach (destruction, district access, turret support). Purchases persist; increasing tiers visibly lengthen the blade, change its color and multiply impact effects. Vector and Breach unlock rooftop traversal and adjoining blocks. Costs grow rather than resetting progress.

Minute 1: a slash throws chrome fragments, a shot detonates concrete, the first upgrade is reachable. Minute 10: larger formations, reflected shield attacks, multiple weapons and movement shortcuts. Minute 30: eight-tier mastery branches, distant districts, high waves and spectacular chained destruction. Empty atmospheric stretches preserve the source's eerie mood between attacks.

Art: symmetrical brutalist blocks, barred windows, wet teal slabs, low amber sun, chrome sculpted hearts and the red floor vent. Procedural world-space triplanar material on architecture. Instanced debris, bounded enemies and quality presets protect frame time. Audio is synthesized. Static Three.js 0.170.0 CDN import is the only runtime dependency.
