# MUD BOGGIN
Inspiration image: fb7dbc82

A one-page design · 24 October 2026

**Fantasy / verb.** Grip a dirty, detailed ATV cockpit under a low silver sky. DRIVE: choose a firm line, lean into rear-wheel slip, punch through reflective water, and fling the backwoods into the air.

**Genre.** Free-roaming off-road driving playground. Start in motion with one throttle press; no intro. First-person handlebars, instant chase camera. A compact, continuous riding ground contains beginner bogs, logging ridges, a clay quarry and flooded swamp. Everything visible is reachable; machinery makes difficult terrain manageable rather than opening arbitrary doors.

**Loop.** Cross route gates and smash rotten obstacles → bank distance, splash and climb cash → build a specialized rig → tackle deeper mud and steeper lines.

**Handling and feedback.** Torque fights terrain drag; steering slips more at speed and in water. Suspension follows uneven ground, tires throw instanced clods, water rings expand, ruts persist for the session and slow later crossings. Impact kick, debris, engine pitch and splash noise communicate grip. Reset is instantaneous. Orange pairs of flags teach a looping route without text.

**Progression.** Six linked branches, eight tiers each: torque, gearing, tire width, suspension, light chassis and waterproofing. Costs grow geometrically. Torque needs gearing investment at high tiers; wide tires trade speed for flotation; suspension and lightness improve climbs and jumps. Tires physically widen, suspension lifts the rig, upgraded body colors and snorkel communicate investment. Milestones unlock differential lock (tires 2), winch (torque 3), paddle flotation (waterproofing 3), quarry capability and deep swamp capability. Garage route income accrues while riding. The long-term goal is a fully built expedition ATV; no prestige resets.

**Quick play.** Instant Bog Run switches to a stock rig on a fixed seeded crossing, scores progress toward the far treeline, ends on crossing or 60 seconds; retry instantly. Main save remains separate.

**Minute 1.** Start spraying mud, pass the first gate, smash a fence, afford an initial upgrade. **Minute 10.** Tune traction versus speed, unlock winch and diff, ride quarry ramps. **Minute 30.** Build high-tier combinations and master swamp crossings with better payouts.

**Art / performance.** Chocolate churned terrain, silver puddles, muted spring greens and charcoal ATV plastics. Procedural world-space triplanar terrain shading; sculpted cowling, bent tubular handlebars, display, grips, cables, mirror and treaded wheels. Fogged instanced forest, pooled particles and ruts, low/medium/high quality. Synthesized audio, local save, static Three.js build.
