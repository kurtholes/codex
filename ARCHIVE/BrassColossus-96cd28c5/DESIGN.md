# Brass Colossus
Inspiration image: 96cd28c5

**Fantasy / verb.** Pilot a towering sun-etched brass knight through a sunset river valley. Brawl: plant feet, trade punches, brace, parry a red windup, shoulder-charge, winch and throw walking fortresses through their own battlements. Start in striking distance, with no start screen.

**Genre / camera.** Open-world mech melee playground. Low, offset third-person chase camera with huge foreground brass shoulders; wider framing during a grapple. Peach cloud banks, green plains, a reflective winding river, smoky iron silhouettes and blue-lit machinery retain the image’s place.

**Loop.** Spot rival or foundry → break armor and battlements → earn machinery → fit a visible chassis tier → cross harder terrain and fight larger fortress-knights.

**Moment-to-moment.** Three alternating strikes build a heavy finisher. Timed brace counters; held brace absorbs at steam cost. Winch attaches then throws in the facing direction. Rip a stunned rival’s armor into a temporary shield/club. Charges crater soil and topple towers. Armor fragments scatter, damage numbers jump, steam hisses, camera kicks and impacts briefly stop time. Enemies have visible health and red attack telegraphs. Capture foundries by clearing their defenders; defend them from returning rivals while they manufacture salvage and repairs.

**Progression.** Five branches × six permanent tiers: Furnace (heat/damage), Pistons (speed/lunge/climbing), Armor (hull/riverbed), Winch (reach/throw/ripping), Foundry (salvage/repair). At tier three each branch commits to one of two competing systems: blast/condense, ram/stride, bulwark/reflect, throw/ripper, mint/repair. All tiers add visible rings, armor, pistons, reels or chimneys. No prestige resets. Piston II unlocks long lunges, Armor II riverbed traction, Piston IV cliff claws; higher foundries and stronger territory rivals reward combinations. Workshops and blueprints persist locally.

**Free play / quick play.** Roam toward any visible structure; the valley, flooded ruins, canyon foundry and mountain citadel connect without chapter gates. Foundry Rumble is a separate 90-second survival run with Brawler / Runner / Bastion builds, escalating pairs and an arena rebuilt per run. Valley progress stays separate. Replay is one click.

**Minute 1 / 10 / 30.** First minute: punch the opening rival, catch a red windup, smash the nearby wall and fit a furnace ring. Ten: throw larger rivals into towers, capture productive workshops, use long lunges and river traction. Thirty: climb to citadels with a visibly transformed knight, test branch combinations and hold workshops against heavier rivals.

**Delivery.** Procedural assets only; curved armor shells, pipes, sun engravings and rivets, not proxy boxes for the focal armor. World-space triplanar surface detail. Pinned Three.js 0.170.0 from jsDelivr, no other runtime libraries; synthesized sound, no accounts or analytics. Static dist alongside source. Instanced debris/rocks, distant mech LOD, adaptive low/medium/high. Test-only controls stripped from dist. A three-minute headless combat test records real and simulation time, low FPS, errors, four screenshots and earned upgrade; additional checks cover persistence, abilities, modes and production stripping. Report measured limits honestly.
