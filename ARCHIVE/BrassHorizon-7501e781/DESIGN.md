# Brass Horizon
Inspiration image: 7501e781

## Fantasy / verb
Your hands belong on that black ribbed grip. A vast armed dirigible moves with weight beyond the broad windows; ivory needles swing, engine lamps breathe, and a cannon's recoil rattles the brass cockpit. **Pilot** a flying siege engine through a warm, smoky industrial sky.

## Genre / loop
Open-world airship destruction sandbox with long incremental progression.
**Explore → shell factories → chain collapsing structures → pull in salvage → refit → fly higher and farther.**

## First ten seconds
Already airborne, moving toward a close, fragile refinery. A small reticle points at red pressure tanks. Hold fire and watch a slow tracer shell hit, blast, break buildings, and shower salvage. WASD pilots, Q/E changes altitude, mouse aims, Space fires. No mandatory menu or exposition. Two compact mode tabs offer a persistent frontier and a five-minute sortie with three loadouts.

## World and feedback
Seamless floating foundry islands, chimneys, suspension bridges, gantry cranes, amber windows and distant zeppelins. Near structures consist of separate breakable beams, tanks, roofs and smokestack sections; blast propagation can collapse entire blocks. Salvage visibly streams back to the ship. Instanced debris tumbles and fades. Cannon kick, brief hit-stop, camera shake, burst light, impact rings, big reward numbers and layered synthesized engine / cannon / metal sounds make every shot tactile. Turrets retaliate; ramming and storms stress the envelope. A wreck costs momentum and hull, not the entire campaign.

## Progression / movement
Six branches, eight tiers each (48 purchases), with increasing costs and district dependencies:
- Siege: heavier shells, wider blast, twin barrels, then colossal siege rounds.
- Volatile: tank chains, incendiary bursts, increasingly broad secondary blasts.
- Salvage: attraction radius, visible collection drones, yield and automatic collection.
- Engines: speed, reverse thrust, side jets, emergency lift, additional visible engine pods.
- Envelope: hull, regeneration, armor ribs, reinforced lamps and collision resistance.
- Storm: ceiling and storm resistance, opening foundry canyons, upper shipyards and lightning belt.
Purchases are possible in flight; no prestige resets. Districts reward progressively more salvage and resist weak guns. First major upgrade adds twin cannon barrels and a visibly stronger salvo. Changes affect cockpit attachments, projectile size/color, movement and available height. Save campaign salvage, tiers and lifetime destruction locally. Quick-play is separate, timed, replayable and supplies strong loadouts immediately.

## Why keep playing?
**Minute 1:** steer an enormous craft, crush a nearby block, chase a salvage stream, buy a first tier.
**Minute 10:** twin siege cannons and drones turn deliberate shots into whole-island chains; lateral thrust lets the player thread towers.
**Minute 30:** the upper shipyards and storm belt offer rich armored factories; stacked weapons and lift upgrades transform both silhouette and flight. A distant visible island remains an invitation.

## Presentation / constraints
Mahogany, near-black iron, aged gold, ivory and amber, with teal smoky distance. A modeled cockpit dominates the lower frame: turned brass plinth, ribbed grip, independently animated levers, needle instruments, bolts, pipes, wheel and lamps. World-space triplanar procedural noise on large surfaces. Stylized procedural geometry throughout; no downloaded art. Three.js 0.170.0 pinned jsDelivr module, bundled locally for zero runtime network. Static source and dist; 60 fps target with low/medium/high pixel and particle budgets. Dev instrumentation is appended only by a development server and excluded from dist. Automated 180-second real-time core-loop test and four checkpoints; report measured software-rendered fps honestly.
