# HAT HAVOC
Inspiration image: a5d8e4f8

## Fantasy & verb
Be the impossible flower-hatted showman: surf a levitating paving slab above a crooked Victorian market and **hurl** slow, enormous pollen balls through its architecture. Black ragged tailoring, crimson ribbon and a towering bouquet make the player instantly recognizable. Amber lanterns, ochre masonry and teal fog preserve the source's theatrical contrast.

## Genre & loop
Open-district aerial destruction sandbox with persistent incremental upgrades.
Launch → steer and hurl → topple market structures → magnetize coins → grow hat/slab powers → fly into richer districts.

## Play, immediately
The camera starts behind the showman, facing fragile stalls. WASD steers relative to view; mouse drag/arrows turn; click fires; Space rises; Shift descends. No intro. Targets glow, impacts flash, numbers rise, timber tumbles and flowers burst. Three-minute quick play grants flight and heavy pollen immediately, with periodic free upgrade choices and a final replay panel.

## Progression
Eight branches, eight tiers each: pollen mass (orb silhouette/damage), thorn penetration (targets per shot), bloom radius (radial cascades), chain reaction (recursive market damage), magnet (pickup radius), familiars (orbiting flower collectors/passive income), slab surfing (speed/dash), and flight (altitude/roof punches). Purchases grow bouquet and slab accents. First surfing purchase unlocks dash; flight tiers unlock bazaar altitude, elevated galleries and bell-tower airspace. Four contiguous districts rise in height, density, reward and durability; roam freely, then earn enough power to dismantle the far districts. No prestige reset. Save coins and every tier locally.

## Minute 1 / 10 / 30
1: immediate juicy shots, crumbling stalls, swirling rewards and first purchase. 10: penetration and bloom chain reactions clear facades; dash opens fast strafing routes and familiars collect the aftermath. 30: a huge bouquet, giant projectiles and sustained flight flatten tower clusters for escalating prices. A renewable market keeps the toy alive.

## Feel & budget
Slow luminous projectiles communicate mass; kick, brief hit-stop, synthesized thumps, splinter instances, petal confetti and floating score communicate impact. Architecture breaks in authored chunks; supporting destruction cascades upper chunks. Shared geometry/materials, bounded instanced debris/crowds, fog and quality-dependent pixel ratios target integrated GPUs. World-space triplanar stone grain avoids stretched surfaces. No external assets or services beyond the exact pinned Three.js module.
