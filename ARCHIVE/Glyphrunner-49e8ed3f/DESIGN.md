# GLYPHRUNNER
Inspiration image: 49e8ed3f

Become the scroll-bearing silhouette in an immense storm-lit stone maze. The core verb is **run → vault → smash**. Third-person exploration/action best preserves the image's scale while making its masonry a physical playground. Lookout pillars pull the camera overhead without interrupting play.

**Loop:** Chase green wisps → evade guardians → shoulder through stone → awaken a distant beacon → grow a permanent glyph constellation → reach farther.

The first corridor is open, mushrooms frame a low breakable wall, and the first beacon is visible beyond it. Movement begins immediately; a compact icon strip teaches sprint, charge and vault. Scroll ribbon, cyan visited mushrooms and tall beacon spires provide physical navigation. No riddles or dialogue.

The cloak is a shaped, layered hood and flared mantle covered in glyph marks; its luminous scroll lights the hand. Charcoal engraved masonry, moss seams, cyan fungi, teal volumetric-looking haze, distant jagged silhouettes and occasional lightning retain the reference's palette. World-space triplanar procedural stone prevents stretched surfaces.

**Progression:** Five interconnected branches with eight ranks each: momentum (damage, wider charge, cascading fracture), grace (vault, chain vault, wall-run), thread (reach, tether, air control), ward (parry, vitality, stomp), network (trail longevity, beacon income, energy multiplier). Ranks cost increasingly more; every purchase changes trail, cloak trim, scroll size or movement. Branch milestones unlock traversal into flooded lower lanes, fungal gardens, elevated causeways and outer maze. All ranks remain; no prestige. Beacons can be awakened in any order and supply passive energy.

**Minute 1:** sprint through a wall in a spray of glyph stones, follow the scroll ribbon, ignite a beacon. **Minute 10:** combine charge and chain vaults, dodge guardians, buy branching upgrades and find elevated routes. **Minute 30:** cross the outer maze using tether, wall-run and stomp; a brilliant scroll, long ribbon and broad cascading destruction express accumulated power.

Quick play is a compact one-life run with an immediately visible escape spire, random movement pickups, and advancing fog. Main mode autosaves currency, upgrades and beacons locally. Destruction creates temporary rubble ramps; instanced fragments settle and fade to keep rendering bounded. Low/medium/high presets scale pixels, particles and decoration. Target: 60 fps integrated laptop graphics.
